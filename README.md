SMS Privacy Policy
Institute for TMJ, Headache & Sleep, PLLC
Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Institute for TMJ, Headache & Sleep, PLLC (“Institute for TMJ, Headache & Sleep,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information related to our text messaging communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from us, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name
- Appointment and scheduling information
- Communication preferences
- Message interaction information, such as delivery confirmations or responses

2. How We Use SMS Communications

We use SMS messaging to communicate with patients regarding:
- Appointment scheduling
- Appointment reminders
- Follow-up communication related to care
- Customer care and patient support communications

SMS messages will not be used for marketing purposes unless you provide separate express consent where required by law.

3. Consent to Receive SMS Messages

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from Institute for TMJ, Headache & Sleep, PLLC. Consent is not a condition of receiving medical treatment or services.

Message frequency may vary depending on your appointments and communication needs.

4. Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:
STOP

After you opt out, you may receive a final confirmation message confirming your unsubscribe request.

For assistance, reply:
HELP

Or contact us at:
info@institutefortmj.com

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with service providers that assist us in delivering SMS communications, provided they maintain the confidentiality and security of your information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.
